    Roadmap to the platinum — about 200 hours

    Built from the official descriptions and PowerPyx's roadmap (18 Nov 2026). One playthrough is enough: there is free-roam after the story but no mission replay and no New Game+. Cheats disable trophies and saving, so keep them off for the whole run.

      The 100% checklist, in brief

      The checklist unlocks after the story (Progress > Stats > 100% Completion). Its thresholds are lenient — roughly 60% true completion: 70 of 80 challenges, 200 of 277 vehicles scanned, 100 of 255 animals studied through binoculars, 50 of 66 weapons, 300 stunts. Most of it overlaps with other trophies, so the story stage already starts it. Source: PowerPyx.

      Missable trophies — and the moments to watch

      As of 19 Nov 2026 only one trophy is flagged as possibly missable by the guides that have played the game. Everything else can be finished in free-roam after the story — but there is no mission replay, so one-time moments inside missions deserve care while you are in them.

      Watch list — not missable, but one-time moments

      These trophies are not flagged missable, yet each is tied to a single moment or a one-way choice. Do them when the moment appears rather than assuming a replay.
